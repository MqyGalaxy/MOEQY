We hope that more people who use other languages can discover our work and share it together.
<!--more-->

MOEQY hopes to get help from more people who are proficient in Chinese and other languages. If you would like your language to appear in MOEQY, please visit the link below:

https://www.moeqy.com/en/help/translate/

You can help us translate unfinished parts, or correct our translation errors.

The languages we are currently planning are as follows:
 - 简体中文 (Simplified Chinese)
 - English
 - 繁體中文 (Traditional Chineses)
 - 日本語 (Japanese)

If your language is not in the plan, don't worry, you can also go to the link to contact us!
